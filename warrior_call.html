Questions before you begin?

Before you answer the call

Does it cost anything to answer the call?

No. Answering the call and your clarity call are free. The Intensive is an investment, and I share the details and payment options personally on your call.

Is this group coaching or private coaching?

Private. Every session is one on one with me by video call. You are one of 100 women answering the call this year, but your coaching is yours alone.

Why only 100 women?

Because this is personal. I coach every woman myself, so I limit each month to 9 new women and the whole year to 100.

What if the month I want is full?

You will be offered the next open month. Seats are given in the order women say yes.

Why say yes by December 31?

Only 9 women can begin each month, and January seats are claimed first. Claim your seat by December 31, 2026, and your printed planner arrives before the New Year so you can start 2027 ready. After that, seats continue to fill month by month until all 100 are claimed.

What if my life is a mess right now?

Then you are exactly who this is for. You do not have to have it together to answer. You only have to be willing.

How does the Blessing Train work?

When a sister you invite joins the Intensive, she receives 10% off, and you receive 10% off your own Intensive. Each woman can receive one 10% blessing. Then she is free to pass it on.
