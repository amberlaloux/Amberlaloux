My Story
I didn't start out as a coach.
I started out as a woman on her knees.

This is the story of 17 years of prayer, a divided home, a God who answers, and a woman He called out of silence to speak life to the world.

Who Is Amber LaLoux?

First and foremost I am a child of the Most High God. I am a wife, a mother, and an entrepreneur — and everything I do flows from that foundation of faith.

For 17 years I prayed for my husband to come to the Lord. Alone. In silence. In a home filled with tension, divided loyalties, and the quiet grief of watching my children grow up caught between two worlds — mommy's faith and daddy's resistance.

I know what it feels like to be overwhelmed, exhausted, and quietly losing yourself in the middle of motherhood and marriage. I know what it feels like to question God's timing, to wrestle with Him in the dark, and to keep standing anyway.

And I know what it feels like when God finally moves. When the miracle comes. When the testimony is born.

This is that story.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord — plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."

— Jeremiah 29:11

"She is clothed with strength and dignity, and she laughs without fear of the future."

— Proverbs 31:25
The Hard Years

My children didn't know which voice to follow. There was anger in our home. Confusion. And in the middle of all of it, I began to do something I am not proud of — I began to resent God. I questioned Him. I wrestled with Him.

Lord, why is this taking so long? Why do my children have to grow up without the father they deserve? Why won't you move?

And then God spoke something to me that changed everything.

In my most desperate moments of prayer, carrying the spiritual weight of my entire family alone, I found myself trying to be everything to my children — their protector, their spiritual covering, their everything. And God lovingly corrected me:

"They are not your children to protect. They are Mine. And I will protect them."

I had to release my children into the hands of God. I had to trust that His timing — even when it felt impossibly slow — was perfect. I had to keep standing, keep praying, keep believing, even when nothing around me looked like it was changing.

The Miracle

And then my daughter turned 18.

She got to witness something that I had been believing for nearly two decades — she got to see her daddy change. The man who prays with her now is not the same man who raised her as a little girl. And my daughter knows that. She has seen it with her own eyes. She will carry that testimony with her for the rest of her life.

My husband came to the Lord. God answered. Every hard year was worth it.

And it is always better in His time, not ours.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

— Romans 8:28
The Calling

But God wasn't finished with me yet.

In the middle of all those years of waiting and praying, God began to speak to something else — something I had buried under years of shyness, fear, and insecurity. He told me I had a voice. That He had created me to make a global impact. That there were women around the world who needed to hear what He had built in me through the fire.

I was terrified. I am naturally quiet. Reserved. The last person you would expect to pick up a microphone and speak to a room full of people. But God doesn't call the qualified — He qualifies the called. And He called me.

So I got over myself. I got over my fears. I got over my insecurities. I picked up the microphone and I went wherever the Lord told me to go.

And that is how this ministry was born.

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind."

— 2 Timothy 1:7
Today

Today I am a certified biblical health and life coach, a global best-selling author, and a women's ministry leader who has been featured on CBS, NBC, FOX, ABC, and the Associated Press. I lead a virtual women's ministry serving women internationally, including women in Pakistan who are hungry for the Word of God.

But more than any title — I am a woman who knows what it is to wait on God in the dark. To pray when nothing seems to be changing. To stand in the gap for your children and your husband and your home when you feel completely alone.

And I am here to tell you — God hears you. He sees you. And it is always better in His time, not ours.
