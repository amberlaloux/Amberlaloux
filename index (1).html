Your Questions Answered

I Know You Might Be Wondering...

What exactly happens on the Discovery Call?

It's a free, pressure-free 30-minute conversation — just you and Amber. She'll listen to where you are, what you're carrying, and what you're hoping for. She'll share how The Intensive works and whether it feels like the right fit. No pressure. No sales pitch. Just two women talking honestly about your life and your next step.

How does payment work?

You can pay in full or choose the flexible 5-payment plan. All payments are processed securely through PayPal. Investment details are shared warmly and openly on your Discovery Call — there are no surprises.

What if I've never done coaching before?

Most of Amber's clients have never worked with a coach before. The Intensive is designed to meet you exactly where you are — no experience needed, no perfect spiritual life required. Just a willing heart and a desire to change.

How is this different from a Bible study or church group?

A Bible study is wonderful — but it was never designed to address the specific, personal chaos in YOUR home. This is private, personalized, and built entirely around your life, your struggles, and your season. Every session, every plan, every prayer is tailored specifically to you. There is no one-size-fits-all here.

Is it okay to charge for biblical coaching? I thought Christians shouldn't take money for ministry.

Such a beautiful and honest question — and one Amber asked herself too. Scripture is actually clear on this: "The worker deserves his wages" (Luke 10:7), and "the one who receives instruction in the word should share all good things with their instructor" (Galatians 6:6). Amber is not charging for the gospel — which is freely given. She is charging for her time, her expertise, her certification, and 17 years of walking through fire so you don't have to walk through it alone. God called her to this work. And He will provide the investment for the woman He has called to receive it.

What if I'm not sure I can commit to 90 days right now?

That is exactly what the Discovery Call is for. Amber will help you discern whether the timing is right and answer every question you have with honesty and grace. There is no pressure — only clarity. Book the call and let God show you what's next.
