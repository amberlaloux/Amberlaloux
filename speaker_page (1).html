Meet the Speaker

A Voice God Called Out of Silence.

Amber LaLoux didn't start out as a speaker. She was naturally quiet, reserved, and terrified of public speaking. But God had other plans.

After 17 years of praying for her husband to come to the Lord — standing in faith alone, raising children in a spiritually divided home, and wrestling with God in her most desperate moments — God told her she had a voice for the world. That He had created her to make a global impact.

So she got over her fears, picked up the microphone, and went wherever the Lord called her to go. Today she is a global best-selling author, certified biblical life coach, and women's ministry leader whose message has reached women internationally.

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind."

— 2 Timothy 1:7
Speaking Topics

Messages That Move Rooms.

Every message Amber brings is rooted in scripture, drawn from personal experience, and delivered with the warmth of a mama who has walked through fire and come out the other side with tools, wisdom, and a testimony.

01
Faith & Spiritual Warfare

How to pray powerfully, fast strategically, and stand in faith until your breakthrough comes — even when nothing around you looks like it's changing. Amber shares from 17 years of personal intercession.

"The effective, fervent prayer of a righteous person accomplishes much." — James 5:16

02
Biblical Health for Moms

God's design for your body, mind, and spirit — practical wellness rooted in scripture, designed for the overwhelmed mama who is running on empty and ready to feel alive again.

"Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit?" — 1 Corinthians 6:19

03
Marriage & Standing in Faith

How to intercede for your husband, walk in your God-given authority as a wife, and trust God's timing even when your marriage feels impossible. A message born from 17 years of believing.

"Love is patient, love is kind... it always hopes, always perseveres." — 1 Corinthians 13:4-7

04
Speak Life — The Power of Your Words

How to harness the power of life in your tongue — breaking cycles of negative self-talk, cursing your children, and speaking death over your family — and replacing them with declarations of life and faith.

"The power of life and death is in the tongue." — Proverbs 18:21

05
The Peaceful Home

Practical tools and biblical principles to eliminate chaos, silence decision fatigue, and restore the peace in your home that the world is trying so hard to steal from you and your children.

"Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you." — John 14:27

06
Called Out of Your Shell

For the woman who knows God has called her but fear, shyness, and insecurity are keeping her silent. Amber's personal testimony of going from terrified introvert to global speaker and author.

"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works." — Ephesians 2:10
