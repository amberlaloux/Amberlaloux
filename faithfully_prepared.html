You do not need a bunker. You need a plan.

Maybe you have watched the news and felt that knot in your stomach. Maybe you have stood in your pantry and wondered how long your family would really last if the lights went out tonight. Maybe you want to prepare, but every prepper video feels expensive, overwhelming, and full of fear.

Mama, preparing is not living in fear. It is stewardship. Joseph stored grain in the years of plenty so his family could eat in the years of famine. You can do the same for your household, one jar of rice and one gallon of water at a time.

“For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.”

— 2 Timothy 1:7 (KJV)

The heart of this course

God's Word is the most important supply in your home

“It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.”

— Matthew 4:4 (KJV)

You can fill every shelf in your pantry and still lie awake afraid. Water, rice, and lamp oil will carry your family through a hard week, but only God's Word will carry your heart. That is why this course begins in Scripture before it ever mentions a shopping list.

In Before You Begin, you will cover your home in prayer, anoint your doorposts, pray over your husband and children, and build a daily habit of time in the Word. When the lights go out, the verses you have hidden in your heart, and in your children's hearts, are the one supply no one can take from you.
