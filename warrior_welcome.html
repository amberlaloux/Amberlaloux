Dear Warrior,

You did it. You heard the call, and you answered. I want you to know how much that matters. So many women feel that stirring in their heart and scroll right past it. You didn't.

I know what it may have taken to get here. Maybe your home feels loud and heavy right now. Maybe you've been carrying everyone else and quietly running on empty. Maybe you've wondered if things could ever really change. Sweet sister, they can. I've lived the chaos and the burnout, and I've watched God bring peace, order, and joy back into my home. He is ready to do the same in yours.

Here's what happens next. I'll read your answers and pray over them personally. Then I'll reach out to talk with you about your next step and your start month.

Until then, everything on this page is for you. Listen to the prayer I recorded just for you. Turn up your Warrior Call song. Put your declaration cards where you'll see them every day, and speak them out loud even on the days you don't feel it yet. And if a sister comes to mind, send her a Praying for You card.

You were never meant to fight alone. You've got this, Mama. God's got you, and so do I.

With love and prayers,
