Children's Book · Healing & Protection

Being Brave

by Joseph Woodley

A gentle, sensitive resource that helps mamas talk to their children about sexual abuse, whether a child has been hurt or is simply being taught what to do if they ever are. Through the voice of his childhood self, Joseph extends a hand to help children move from pain, shame, and fear toward healing and protection alongside safe adults. Every word is delicately written and age-appropriate, giving little hearts a layer of defense against fear and shame.
